Endless Labyrinth Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Endless Labyrinth handles data. The game is developed and published by NinjaJay Games.

Information We Collect

Endless Labyrinth does not require a user account, login, name, email address, or password. The game may save local gameplay preferences and progress on your device, such as language, settings, inventory, and score-related information.

Advertising

Endless Labyrinth uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to show rewarded and interstitial ads. Google and its partners may process data such as advertising ID, device information, app interaction data, diagnostics, and approximate location for advertising, fraud prevention, security, analytics, and service improvement purposes.

You can learn more about how Google uses data here: Google Advertising Policies.

Internet Connection

Endless Labyrinth requires an internet connection for ad-supported gameplay and connection checks. The game may request a lightweight public file named health.txt to verify that an internet connection is available.

Third-Party Services

The game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. Third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

Endless Labyrinth is not specifically directed to children under 13. If the target audience or content rating changes in the future, this policy may be updated.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described above.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the game changes, when new services are added, or when store requirements change. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the email address shown on the Google Play Store listing for Endless Labyrinth.
